In early March 2020, I graduated from the YMCA Japanese school, ending a year and a half of language study. I had planned to continue to graduate school, but the sudden pandemic made everyone uneasy, so I returned to China first. Based on what happened during SARS, I thought I might be able to come back in six months.

The graduation ceremony had to be canceled. Everyone left in a hurry, and a year and a half together ended in a way that still feels sad.

On the flight home, passengers sat apart. Medical staff came by regularly to check our temperatures and disinfect the cabin. Worried about exposure, I did not eat or drink during the flight. I finally arrived at the quarantine hotel after midnight, finished writing down my precautions, and went to sleep safely.

I could not think clearly about the future. My education had stalled while the outside world was in chaos. For the moment, I decided to wait until quarantine ended and go home.
