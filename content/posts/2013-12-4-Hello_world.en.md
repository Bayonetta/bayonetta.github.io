Let me start the very first log with Hello World — it counts as a kind of convention, or you could call it Blog [0] as well.

I had wanted to keep a log for a while, but it always sat scattered in Evernote, waiting for a slow spell when I could tidy it all up.

Now that GitHub Pages exists, I can use an existing template and free hosting to put everything online, with unlimited traffic and unlimited space. That is simply amazing!!

These past few days I have been getting familiar with Jekyll, and it does not feel easy to pick up. There are still subtle differences in the setup experience between Arch Linux and Mac OS X, and comparatively speaking, Mac is the friendlier of the two.

From now on I have to work hard to be a quality content provider!

PS: The name of the blog comes from my favourite song of the same name, with lyrics by Lin Xi and sung by Miriam Yeung. 【丁目】 is a Japanese street name.
