In May 2018, I left an iOS job I had held for almost four years. After a year of studying, I passed the EF level of the J.Test at the beginning of the year. Following several months of messages and paperwork with Mr. Ogawa at YMCA, I finally returned to Kyoto in early October and enrolled in the Kyoto YMCA Japanese school. It felt strange and wonderful to become a student again.

I lived near the Kamo River and Keihan Shichijo Station. There was a McDonald's by the exit, which soon became a regular stop. I quickly became friends with three Taiwanese students in the same building, and we got along well.
