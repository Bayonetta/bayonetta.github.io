In August, three classmates and I decided to learn to drive in Kochi. We chose a driving school in Aki because the price was reasonable. In early October we traveled from Ishikawa through Nagoya and flew to Kochi.

What followed was fifteen intense days of driving lessons. The first week covered skills on the school course, ending with a test for a provisional license. During the second week we drove on public roads and took another test.

Halfway through, we met two Chinese students from Kanazawa University who also came from Ishikawa. Meeting people from a familiar place so far away felt like a coincidence.

We were too busy to explore, except for a few drives to the coast and the foot of the mountains. The scenery was beautiful.

<figure><img src="/images/2025-11-5-4.jpg" alt=""/><figcaption>A rare break to look at the sea</figcaption></figure>

After returning to school, we decided to take the license exam at the Kanazawa center before we forgot what we had learned. On the last weekday of October, we left after six in the morning, changed trains several times, and arrived at 8:30. Two hours after the exam, all four of us had passed.

After another hour of waiting, we had our photos taken and received our licenses. A very full day.
