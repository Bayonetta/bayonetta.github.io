In May 2026, I attended the 17th Annual Meeting of the Asian Association for Algorithms and Computation in Qingdao and gave my second academic presentation.

The funny part was that I had already graduated and could no longer register as a student, so I paid the faculty registration fee. Once I arrived, the staff kept calling me “teacher”, and I had to explain that I was actually a recent graduate.

I also met many students and professors from Japan at the conference, and we had some very enjoyable conversations.
