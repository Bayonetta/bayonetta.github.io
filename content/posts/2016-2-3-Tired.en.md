After the company was acquired last October, we moved to Zhongguancun, Beijing's “Silicon Valley”. We did not have a permanent place for our team, so over the next few months we moved from one Sina office to another. Even a three-family servant could not have changed employers so often.

The main battle was the headline-article feature in Weibo's main client. After the acquisition, I became the only iOS developer in the entire iOS group. The boss paid two former colleagues to help in their spare time, but as the core developer I carried a lot of pressure. I practically lived in a meeting room.

My anxiety finally surfaced in the middle of one night. The physical symptoms became stronger, so I took a week off. After returning, I spent another two months building a standalone app for the headline feature. During that period the company took us skiing at Nanshan. It was only my second time skiing, but it unexpectedly relieved me. I bought all the equipment and went to the slopes whenever the smog allowed.

The anxiety did not disappear. Panic, difficulty breathing, numb hands and feet, and overreacting to ordinary events began to affect daily life. I saw a psychologist and took medication without much improvement. I gradually started thinking about resigning. For now, I could only take one step at a time.
