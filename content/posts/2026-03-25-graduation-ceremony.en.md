In March 2026, I attended JAIST's master's graduation ceremony. It was my second graduation ceremony, and probably the last time I would sit among the audience as a student. The procedure was similar to an entrance ceremony, but the feeling was completely different: then I was uncertain; now I was satisfied.

The previous ceremony was at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in June 2014. I still remember the noise and laughter. The YMCA language-school years later ended without a ceremony because of the pandemic, leaving another regret.

Recently I had felt disconnected from the world. During one of my last lunches at Yuyu, I heard that Zhang Xuefeng had died, which left me deeply moved. Should a person pursue a spectacular rise like a firework, or flow steadily like a long river? Different people will have different answers.
