On December 26, we left school for Hokkaido. One senior who had already graduated flew from Tokyo, and we planned to meet in Sapporo. Ishikawa had even heavier snow than Hokkaido, so our flight was canceled. We had to fly to Fukuoka first and continue to Sapporo the next day. It became an unexpected side trip, although our senior had to spend the first night alone in a guesthouse.

## Sapporo

Once we finally met, the graduation trip began. The soup curry we queued three hours for left a deep impression.

## Otaru and Sapporo

We went to Otaru the next day. I first heard the city's name in the film *Love Letter*. The students born in the 2000s had never heard of it; it was an old-fashioned romance for people like me.

The whole chicken we had for lunch was excellent. The city looked exactly as I remembered it.

<figure><img src="/images/2026-1-3-4.jpg" alt=""/><figcaption>The city looked like the memory</figcaption></figure>

Back in Sapporo, we had a lamb hot pot. Winter really is made for lamb. I missed Beijing's lamb-offal soup.

## Noboribetsu's Hell Valley

We visited a zoo near the station to see penguins, then went to Hell Valley. It smelled terrible the entire way; sulfur really does smell terrible.

<figure><img src="/images/2026-1-3-5.jpg" alt=""/><figcaption>Beautiful, but it smelled</figcaption></figure>

## New Year's in Tokyo

A great Xiaohongshu itinerary led us to stay at a hot-spring facility in Shinjuku, since hotels were difficult to book during New Year's. It turned out well, except for the number of people snoring.
