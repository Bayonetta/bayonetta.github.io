Happy graduation. It was time for the annual water fight.

## The ceremony

In June 2014, four years at Huazhong University of Science and Technology came to an end. The ceremony was shorter than expected: photos, moving the tassel, returning the gown, and it was over before lunch. What I remember more clearly are the days around it: the dorm room emptying little by little, only the technical books I planned to take remaining on the shelf, and people on campus beginning to use “after this” in every sentence.

That evening the central sports field was full of noise. The theme song from *Slam Dunk* pushed the mood to its peak. Then everyone began a “riot parade”: students divided into groups by school and started throwing water at one another. There was no distinction between men's and women's dormitories that night; people who dared to enter a dorm became heroes. Younger students and even passers-by joined in. Nobody slept.

#### Afterword

The next year I learned that it had been the last water festival. The new president canceled the wasteful, absurd tradition. That made sense, but I was also glad that the night had become more memorable.

## The final dinner and karaoke

The next evening, across from Guanggu Gymnasium, we mixed white liquor, beer, and red wine at the hotel. It was chaotic, excessive, and full of feelings. A memorial to our last reckless night.
