In March 2026, I attended the 207th IPSJ SIGAL Workshop on Algorithms in Okayama and gave my first academic presentation. It was also a requirement from my laboratory: I needed one presentation on record in order to graduate.

My supervisor had already asked me to attend AAAC 2026 in May, so SIGAL 207 became a useful rehearsal. Before presenting at another international conference, I could practice explaining my master's work to people outside the laboratory.

It went smoothly, and it was a great experience.

That evening, I had dinner with my supervisors at an izakaya. They kept talking about how AI is affecting academic work, which stayed with me. Programming is no different. The road to the next job is probably going to be difficult.
