I suddenly wanted to take the TOEFL in Macau and make a trip of it. The registration fee was more than 600 RMB cheaper than on the mainland—almost enough to cover the round-trip flights. It seemed like a bargain.

The pandemic was not over and everyone was still nervous, which made travel unusually affordable. As usual, I will let the photos do most of the talking.

Macau felt small; two or three days were enough. I missed the Hainanese chicken rice I had been craving, along with Japanese curry.

Afterward I took a ferry to Shenzhen to visit a friend. Perhaps boats and I are incompatible: the waves were so strong that I had to stand inside the cabin to feel better.

The effort paid off. I finally ate the Hainanese chicken rice I had been thinking about.
